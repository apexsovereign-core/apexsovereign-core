Privacy Notice

Draft revision: 2026-10-27

1. Data we process

Account identifiers, email addresses, authentication claims, billing-order identifiers, ledger entries, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service. Payment card data is handled by PayPal and is not stored by this application.

2. Why we process it

To authenticate users, create and capture PayPal orders, verify webhooks, mint CU idempotently, prevent fraud, maintain audit integrity, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Access tokens and secrets

The billing interface accepts a Supabase access token for the current browser session and stores it in sessionStorage. Server secrets such as PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET, SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY, and LEDGER_HMAC_KEYS_JSON must never be shipped to the browser.

4. Retention

Ledger and payment records are retained according to accounting, tax, and dispute-resolution requirements. Retention periods must be finalized by counsel and the operator.

5. Processors

PayPal processes payments. Supabase/PostgreSQL hosts the ledger. Hosting and observability providers may process logs. A current subprocessor list must be maintained before launch.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port personal data. A verified request channel must be established before launch.
