# Data Center Supplier LOI — DRAFT TEMPLATE

**Status:** Non-binding Letter of Intent (draft for counsel review)
**Entity:** ApexSovereign.ai, Inc. (Delaware C-Corp, pending incorporation)

## Purpose
Invite Tier-3 data centers and colocation providers to list idle GPU cluster capacity (H100, B200, A100) on the ApexSovereign.ai compute exchange at zero upfront cost.

## Key Terms (draft — subject to counsel review)
- **Zero upfront fees:** Suppliers pay no listing or onboarding fees.
- **Revenue share:** Suppliers receive a percentage of compute revenue generated from routed workloads.
- **No exclusivity:** Suppliers may continue selling capacity through other channels.
- **Term:** Month-to-month, terminable with 30 days notice.
- **Data handling:** Workload data remains ephemeral; no persistent storage on supplier hardware.

## Next Steps
- Supplier reviews and returns signed LOI.
- ApexSovereign.ai provisions supplier credentials via the inbound agent pipeline.
- Capacity goes live after hardware verification and benchmark attestation.
